Bedrock Bound: a Minecraft mining game

Somewhere under the grass, under the stone, the deepslate and even the Nether, the Heart of the World is waiting. Grab your wooden pickaxe and dig. Every ore you bring back to the Trader buys a better pickaxe, a bigger backpack, a brighter lantern or tougher armor, so each trip takes you deeper than the last.

What is down there

  • Coal, copper, iron, lapis, redstone, gold, diamonds and emeralds, each needing the right pickaxe.
  • Dark caves where creepers wait for you. Light them with torches.
  • Lava that flows into your tunnels, gravel that falls on your head and hidden TNT.
  • A wall of obsidian, then the Nether: quartz, glowstone, magma cubes and Ancient Debris for a netherite pickaxe.
  • Treasure chests, 12 advancements, and the Heart of the World 300 m down.

Controls

Walk with A/D (Q/D on AZERTY) or the arrows and push into a block to dig it. S digs down, W or Space jumps and climbs. Hold the left mouse button to dig any block in reach. Ladders are placed for you when you climb a shaft; click one or press R to take it back. 1 to 6 picks an item, F uses it, E opens the trade at the surface stall. It also works on phones and tablets with the touch buttons.

Your world saves itself in your browser, so you can come back and keep digging.
